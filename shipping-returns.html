Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery times below begin once an order has shipped.

Standard

2–5 business days · Free over ₦50,000, otherwise ₦2,500

Express

1–2 business days · ₦6,000 flat rate, select cities

International

5–14 business days · Rates calculated at checkout

Returns & Exchanges

We want every piece to fit right. If it doesn't, here's how to send it back:

  1. Contact our support team within 14 days of delivery with your order number.
  2. We'll send a return label and instructions by email.
  3. Pack the item unworn, unwashed, with all original tags attached.
  4. Once received and inspected, we process your refund or exchange within 5–7 business days.

Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout are not eligible for return or exchange. Gift cards are non-refundable.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging — we'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.
