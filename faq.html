Frequently Asked Questions

Sizing

What sizes does Kora offer?
Adult apparel runs XS through XXL. Kids' pieces are sized by age, from 4–5 years through 12–13 years. Bottoms use waist sizing (e.g. 28"–36"). Each product page lists exactly which sizes are currently in stock.
Does Kora run true to size?
Hoodies and crewnecks are cut oversized by design — if you prefer a snug fit, size down. Bottoms and jackets run true to standard measurements. Detailed measurements are available on request via our contact form.
Can I exchange for a different size?
Yes — unworn items with tags attached can be exchanged within 14 days of delivery. See our Shipping & Returns page for the full process.

Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery within Nigeria takes 2–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 business days) is available at checkout in select cities. International shipping timelines are shown at checkout based on destination.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. Order status can also be checked by contacting our support team with your order number.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, Kora ships worldwide. Duties and taxes for international orders are calculated at checkout and may be charged separately by your local customs authority.

Returns

What is your return policy?
We accept returns on unworn, unwashed items with original tags within 14 days of delivery. Final sale items (marked at checkout) are not eligible for return.
How do refunds work?
Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
