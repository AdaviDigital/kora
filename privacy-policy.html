Privacy Policy

Kora Streetwear ("Kora", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide when placing an order, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting support — name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
  • Order information including items purchased, sizes, and payment confirmation (we do not store full card numbers — payments are processed by our PCI-compliant payment provider).
  • Usage data such as pages visited and general device/browser information, used to improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To send drop alerts and marketing emails, only if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve site performance and security.

Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate — payment processors, shipping carriers, and email delivery services — under contracts that require them to protect your data. We never sell your personal information.

Data security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit and follow security best practices to protect information stored on our systems.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting hello@korastreetwear.com.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your cart working and optional analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to our contact page.
