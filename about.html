Founded 2019

Streetwear, stitched with intent.

Kora started as two people, one sewing machine and a refusal to ship anything that felt disposable.

Where it started

A pattern before a print

Most streetwear labels start with a graphic. Kora started with a block pattern — the dropped shoulder, the hood ratio, the cuff tension — because fit is the thing people actually feel every day. The prints came after we were happy with how the fabric moved.

Today Kora is worn across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, but the process hasn't changed: small numbered drops, tested on real bodies, refined before the next run.

Milestones

How we got here

2019

Two people, one sewing machine

Kora launches from a one-room studio with a single hoodie pattern, sold directly to friends and a small Instagram following.

2021

First numbered drop

Kora moves to a drop model — small, dated releases instead of a constant catalogue — and sells out its first 200-unit run in 48 hours.

2023

Kids & family line

Requests from customers buying for their children lead to the launch of Kora Junior, scaled down without scaling down the quality.

2026

Drop 04: Obsidian

Our biggest release yet — heavier fleece, a refined flourish motif, and the first full accessories line.

What we stand for

Three rules we don't break

Fabric first

We choose gsm and fibre blend before we choose a colourway. If the hand-feel isn't right, the piece doesn't ship.

Small runs, honestly numbered

We tell you exactly how many units exist. When a size is gone, we say so instead of quietly restocking under a new name.

Design for real bodies

Every pattern is tested across a real size range before it goes to production — not just the sample size.
